Initials avatar — used for AMs, marketplace editors, and clients. No photos in the system; always initials on a flat color.

```jsx
<Avatar initials="SM" color="slate" />
<Avatar initials="LF" color="brand" size="lg" />
```

Assign each person a stable `color` so they're recognizable across screens. Sizes: `sm` (inline in tables/meta rows), `md` (default), `lg` (headers / rail user).
